Company Profile & Positioning

Specialist Façade Execution for Commercial Developments.

GLASS FAB INDIA delivers turnkey architectural glazing, aluminium systems, and building envelope solutions engineered for structural longevity, environmental efficiency, and architectural distinction.

We operate exclusively in the B2B sector, partnering directly with real estate developers, main civil contractors, façade contractors, EPC companies, architects, and institutional project owners.
